# Jessica-Emberly-Photography
A page for SEO that contains information about the photographer Jessica Emberly.

## About
Jessica Emberly is an american photographer from Kansas, who specializes in horrorifying visuals. Jessica uses her photography as an escape from the world.

She learned about her favorite horror film at a young age, and by the film being SAW (2004), she started fantasizing about unaliving what she deemed aweful people, and to ease that, she lives out her fantasies through her photography, which she considers her art.
